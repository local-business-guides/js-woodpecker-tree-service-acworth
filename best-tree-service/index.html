What to ask before work begins

A good hiring conversation should leave you clear about what is being considered. You can use this short checklist when you contact a tree service:

  • Describe the tree or branches you want addressed.
  • Explain what you want the finished result to be.
  • Ask whether the work being discussed involves trimming, removal, or another tree service need.
  • Request the price up front and confirm what work it covers.
  • Talk through any concerns about the tree’s location on your property.

Avoid agreeing to work before you understand its scope and price. Don’t try to handle hazardous cutting yourself, especially if you’re unsure how the tree or branches may move. If the situation seems unsafe, keep people away from the area and contact a professional to discuss it.
